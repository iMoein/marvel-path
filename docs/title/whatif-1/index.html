چه می‌شد اگر؟ — فصل ۱ — What If...? — Season 1

این انیمیشن جهان‌های موازی را با این پرسش بررسی می‌کند: اگر رویدادهای آشنای MCU طور دیگری رخ می‌دادند چه می‌شد؟

سال: 2021 · 9 قسمت

بازیگران و نقش‌ها

  • Jeffrey Wright — The Watcher (voice)

قسمت‌ها

  1. What If... Captain Carter Were the First Avenger? — When Steve Rogers is seriously injured, Peggy Carter becomes the world’s first Super Soldier.
  2. What If... T'Challa Became a Star-Lord? — The rough-and-tumble space pirates known as the Ravagers abduct T’Challa instead of Peter Quill.
  3. What If... The World Lost Its Mightiest Heroes? — Nick Fury struggles to launch The Avengers when candidates are targeted by a serial killer.
  4. What If... Doctor Strange Lost His Heart Instead of His Hands? — A grief-stricken Doctor Strange uses the Eye of Agamotto in a dangerous effort to change the past.
  5. What If... Zombies!? — When The Avengers are infected by a zombie plague, surviving heroes search for a cure.
  6. What If... Killmonger Rescued Tony Stark? — An American soldier called Killmonger rescues Tony Stark when his Humvee is attacked in Afghanistan.
  7. What If... Thor Were an Only Child? — Thor, who never learned to be a good hero, throws an out-of-control intergalactic party on Earth.
  8. What If... Ultron Won? — Natasha Romanoff and Clint Barton seek to destroy killer-robot Ultron following a cataclysmic event.
  9. What If... The Watcher Broke His Oath? — The Watcher breaks his oath and recruits heroes from across the multiverse to battle against Ultron.
