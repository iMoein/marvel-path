قسمت‌ها

  1. What If... Nebula Joined the Nova Corps? — In a world where Ronan deposes Thanos long before the events of "Infinity War," Nebula is recruited to join the Nova Corps. Desperate to escape her father's shadow, she sets out to prove her detective chops.
  2. What If... Peter Quill Attacked Earth's Mightiest Heroes? — In 1988 after seeing his mother Meredith die, Peter Quill is abducted by a group of Ravengers led by Yondu Udonta sent by the Celestial planet Ego. However rather than having a change of heart and raising Quill as his own, Yondu gave him to Ego as originally planned. Taking his walkman away, Ego raised him beginning to show his Expansion plan.
  3. What If... Happy Hogan Saved Christmas? — When Justin Hammer lays siege to Avengers Tower during the annual holiday party, there's only one person left on site to stop him - Happy Hogan. But in setting out to prove his hero chops, Iron Man's errand boy may end up transforming himself in more ways than one.
  4. What If... Iron Man Crashed into the Grandmaster? — After his gambit to destroy the Chitauri Fleet during the battle of New York strands him on the planet of Sakaar, Tony Stark's efforts to overthrow The Grandmaster lead to a ceremonial, grand prix-turned-demolition-derby—a death match that could land Tony on the Sakaarian throne.
  5. What If... Captain Carter Fought the Hydra Stomper? — Peggy Carter / Captain Carter believes that the love of her life, Steve Rogers, is long-dead until he is discovered alive as the villainous Hydra Stomper.
  6. What If... Kahhori Reshaped the World? — A Mohawk woman ventures into the waters of the Forbidden Lake to help save her people.
  7. What If... Hela Found the Ten Rings? — Growing weary of Hela's bloodlust, Odin opts to teach his daughter a lesson by stripping her of her powers and banishing her to Earth. But when she lands in ancient China, the seductive lure of the Ten Rings threatens to reawaken the Goddess of Death's villainous appetite.
  8. What If... The Avengers Assembled in 1602? — When Captain Carter is transported to an Elizabethan-era locale that's populated by modernday MCU figures, she'll have to uncover the cause of the temporal anomaly that's mysteriously stranded so many familiar heroes and villains in the year 1602.
  9. What If... Strange Supreme Intervened? — When Captain Carter teams up with an old friend to hunt down vicious Universe Killers terrorizing the Multiverse, she discovers the greatest threat to all existence may be closer than she realizes.
