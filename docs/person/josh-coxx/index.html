Joshua Cox (born August 9, 1965), credited as Josh Coxx, is an American actor. He is perhaps most famous for his work in television. His notable roles include Lieutenant David Corwin on Babylon 5 and Senior Nurse Peter Riggs on Strong Medicine. A 2007 Mercedes commercial featured him. From Wikipedia, the free encyclopedia.
