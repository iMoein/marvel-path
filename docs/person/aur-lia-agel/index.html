Aurélia Agel (Orléans, February 26, 1998) is a French stuntwoman specialising in action and martial arts. She is known for her roles in Fast and Furious 10, Guardians of the Galaxy Vol. 3, and John Wick: Chapter 4. She was considered "the sexiest stuntwoman in the world" and has performed alongside stars Charlize Theron, Milla Jovovich, and Olga Kurylenko. She stood out as the stunt double for Karen Gillan, who plays Nebula in the Marvel Cinematic Universe.
