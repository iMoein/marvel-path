Anjana Vasan

Anjana Vasan؛ بازیگر مرتبط با مرد عنکبوتی: دور از خانه. مشاهده نقش‌ها و فیلموگرافی در مسیر مارول.

Anjana Vasan (born January 31, 1987) is an Indian-born Singaporean actress and singer-songwriter based in London. She is known for her stage work, winning a Laurence Olivier Award, and her role in the Channel 4 sitcom We Are Lady Parts, for which she was nominated a British Academy Television Award. She starred in the Black Mirror episode "Demon 79" in 2023.
