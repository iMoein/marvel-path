Shauna Nicole Kain is a actress of cinema and television American/Canadian. Its most recognized role is that of the mutant Siryn in the movie X-Men 2 about the famous comic series The X Men of Marvel. He repeated the role in the third movie in the saga, X-Men: The Last Stand.oneHe has also acted in movies like Dreamcatcher ( based on the novel The dream hunter of Stephen king) and Red Riding Hood.
