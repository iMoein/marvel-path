Gregory Andrew Rikaart is an American actor. He is best known for playing Kevin Fisher on the television soap opera The Young and the Restless since 2003. Throughout his career, Rikaart has received six nominations for the Daytime Emmy Award for Outstanding Supporting Actor in a Drama Series and won once in 2005.
