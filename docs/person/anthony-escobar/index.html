Anthony Escobar

Anthony Escobar؛ بازیگر مرتبط با لوگان. مشاهده نقش‌ها و فیلموگرافی در مسیر مارول.

Anthony Escobar was an actor, best known for his role in the movie Marvel movie Logan, and for his role in the TV show Better Call Saul. He died in October 2017 at home in El Paso, Texas.
