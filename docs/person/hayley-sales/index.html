Hayley Sales

Hayley Sales؛ بازیگر مرتبط با ددپول ۲. مشاهده نقش‌ها و فیلموگرافی در مسیر مارول.

Hayley Sales is an American actress primarily known for her portrayal as Bruce Willis' girl in the independent film, Corrective Measures, she also had a role in Deadpool 2. She lives in Vancouver, BC, Canada.
