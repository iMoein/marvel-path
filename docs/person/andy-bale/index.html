Andy Bale

Andy Bale؛ بازیگر مرتبط با دکتر استرنج در چندجهانی دیوانگی. مشاهده نقش‌ها و فیلموگرافی در مسیر مارول.

A Detroit native, Andy Bale has been riding the Hudson Valley airwaves for many years and lives in New York's backyard. He is also a citizen of the Marvel Universe, having roles in the blockbuster movies "Spiderman 2" and "Dr. Strange in the Multiverse of Madness". On weekends, Andy plays bass with bands like "Rivals NY" and Jordan Stoner. Other times, he can be found in his bathrobe binging on "What We Do In The Shadows". Andy's a busy guy.
