Jimmy Moneta (born July 20, 2000) is a Polish-American actor. Since beginning his acting career in 2019, he has appeared in a variety of film and television productions and is known for his smaller roles in massive franchises including Sonic, John Wick, Star Wars, the Marvel Cinematic Universe, and the DCEU. He has also gathered an audience online through collaborating with prominent online creators, including David Dobrik and Pokimane. He is the brother of makeup artist Kate Moneta, whose work has been featured in the fashion industry through collaborations with luxury brands including Gucci and Prada; as well as more recent collaborations within the film industry.
