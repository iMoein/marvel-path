Paula Newsome

Paula Newsome؛ بازیگر مرتبط با مرد عنکبوتی: راهی به خانه نیست. مشاهده نقش‌ها و فیلموگرافی در مسیر مارول.

Paula Newsome is an American actress. She starred in several television series, most notably the ABC police drama Women's Murder Club (2007–08). She had recurring roles on NYPD Blue, NCIS, Barry, and Chicago Med. She is best known for her supporting role in the film Spider-Man: No Way Home (2021) and her leading role as Maxine "Max" Roby in the CBS crime drama series CSI: Vegas (2021–2024).
