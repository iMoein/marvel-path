Daley Pearson؛ بازیگر مرتبط با ثور: عشق و تندر. مشاهده نقش‌ها و فیلموگرافی در مسیر مارول.

Daley Pearson is a director and the co-founder of the multi-Emmy® Award-winning Ludo Studio. Pearson is a writer, director, producer, and a multi-Emmy® Award-winning creator and co-founder and director at Ludo Studio. Pearson also starred as Thor's roommate, Darryl, with Chris Hemsworth and Jeff Goldblum in Taika Waititi's Team Thor comedy series for Marvel Studios. He created and wrote the concept script for the new horror feature Talk To Me for A24.
