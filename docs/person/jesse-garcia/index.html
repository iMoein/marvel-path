Jesse García (born December 14, 1982) is a Mexican-American actor. He is known for his roles in Quinceañera and Flamin' Hot, for which he won Imagen Awards Best Actor. Description above from the Wikipedia article Jesse Garcia, licensed under CC-BY-SA, full list of contributors on Wikipedia.
