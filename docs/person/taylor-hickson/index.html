Taylor Hickson؛ بازیگر مرتبط با ددپول. مشاهده نقش‌ها و فیلموگرافی در مسیر مارول.

Taylor Delaney Hickson is a Canadian actress raised in Kelowna, British Columbia. Following a minor part in the movie Blackway, Hickson won a role in the 2016 feature Deadpool. In addition to several subsequent parts in films, she later starred in the television series Aftermath.
