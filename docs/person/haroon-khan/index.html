Haroon Khan؛ بازیگر مرتبط با مرد عنکبوتی: راهی به خانه نیست. مشاهده نقش‌ها و فیلموگرافی در مسیر مارول.

Haroon Khan is an actor of Pakistani descent. After a few years performing in theatre at his high school, he transitioned into background work on various TV shows and movies. His first supporting role was in the acclaimed teen romance film Love, Simon.
