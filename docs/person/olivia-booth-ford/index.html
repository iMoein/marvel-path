Olivia Booth-Ford

Olivia Booth-Ford؛ بازیگر مرتبط با مرد عنکبوتی: روزی نو. مشاهده نقش‌ها و فیلموگرافی در مسیر مارول.

Olivia Booth-Ford is an English actress born on July 3, 2010. She began her career in film and television in the early 2020s, appearing in notable productions such as Netflix's fantasy feature The School for Good and Evil (2022) and the Apple TV+ docudrama series The Enfield Poltergeist (2023). Booth-Ford gained broader visibility joining the main cast of the BBC school drama series Waterloo Road as Leoni Tennant. On the big screen, she portrayed Sara Grey in the Marvel Cinematic Universe feature Spider-Man: Brand New Day (2026).
