Mabel Cadena

Mabel Cadena؛ بازیگر مرتبط با پلنگ سیاه: واکاندا تا ابد. مشاهده نقش‌ها و فیلموگرافی در مسیر مارول.

Mitzi Mabel Martínez Cadena (born 23 September 1990) is a Mexican actress known for her roles in the 2020 films Dance of the 41 and La diosa del asfalto and the 2019 television series Hernán. She made her Hollywood debut as Namora in the 2022 film Black Panther: Wakanda Forever.
