Michael Bruno؛ بازیگر مرتبط با مرد آهنی ۲. مشاهده نقش‌ها و فیلموگرافی در مسیر مارول.

Michael Bruno was born on August 11, 1979 in Rockland, New York, USA as Michael Anthony Bruno. He is an actor and writer, known for Iron Man 2 (2010) and Alvin and the Chipmunks: The Squeakquel (2009).
