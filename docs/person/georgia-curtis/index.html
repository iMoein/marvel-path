Georgia Curtis

Georgia Curtis؛ بازیگر مرتبط با بیوه سیاه. مشاهده نقش‌ها و فیلموگرافی در مسیر مارول.

Georgia Curtis is known for her roles in Black Widow (2020), The Strangers (2019), and West End Girls (2014). Georgia is a mix of Nigerian and Spanish descent and has trained at Beverley Playhouse Acting School in Los Angeles and Identity Drama School in London. She is trained in taekwondo and dance.
