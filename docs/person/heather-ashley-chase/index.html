Heather Ashley Chase؛ بازیگر مرتبط با ددپول. مشاهده نقش‌ها و فیلموگرافی در مسیر مارول.

Heather Ashley Chase was first thrust in front of the cameras, upon winning the "Baby Starkist Tuna" Contest at 6 months old, in 1984. In 1988, at 4 Years of age, she stepped onto a theater stage for a dance recital and was immediately stricken with the "performance bug". Ashley has won several awards in Dance and Theatre, including Best Actress in a comedy for Noises Off, Best Actress in the drama Darkside of the Moon as well as Best Supporting Actress in a comedy, for her role as Lina Lamont in Singing in the Rain as well as Jupiter's Maltz Theatre production of Lend me a Tenor.Ashley was accepted to Yale School of Drama but after only eight months, she landed a lead role in a TV show that moved her back, once again, into the heart of N.Y.C. A few years later, she returned to her studies and transferred to NYU and started performing with Tisch.After landing the lead in an action drama show, known as TaskForce in 2000 and after completing the first season, she then moved to Hollywood California.Within the week, Heather-Ashley landed two lead roles in two different films. One a horror movie and the other a science fiction film.Publications she has been featured in are Sassy Magazine, Palm Beach Magazine, Femme Fatale, Oxygen (Editorial Ad), Yoga Mind Body Spirit Magazine, Palm Beach Gardens Yoga, Travel & Leisure, Ocean Drive Magazine, Boca Magazine, Six Degrees Magazine as well as appearing on stage with Dr. Oz, in 2016 for the Palm Beach Gardens Mind Body Spirit Health Expo. She has made several appearances in and around Palm Beach promoting Holistic Medicine. In 2013 she appeared on Good Morning Miami and the New York CBS Today Show. Heather recently earned her Master's Degree from New York University.- IMDb mini biography by: Universal Management & Talent
