Brian Law

Brian Law؛ بازیگر مرتبط با مرد عنکبوتی: دور از خانه. مشاهده نقش‌ها و فیلموگرافی در مسیر مارول.

Brian Law was born and raised in Toronto, Canada and lived in Miami and Berlin before settling down in London, UK. He has appeared in major projects including 'Spider-man: Far From Home' (Marvel), 'Treason' (Netflix), 'White Dragon' (Amazon/ITV), 'Suspicion' (AppleTV+) and 'Doctor Who' (BBC). Prior to acting, he earned degrees in architecture and public policy.
