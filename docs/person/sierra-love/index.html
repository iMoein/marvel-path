Sierra Love؛ بازیگر مرتبط با نگهبانان کهکشان ۲. مشاهده نقش‌ها و فیلموگرافی در مسیر مارول.

Sierra Love is a native Californian. Sierra grew up in Beaumont, California, and she's the youngest of four children. Sierra is an avid reader, and keeps collections of both fiction and non-fiction. In her free time, she practices Muay Thai and mixed martial arts. She has two cats, named Hephaestus and Persephone.
