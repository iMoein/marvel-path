Darin De Paul is an American voice actor known for his video game performances as Dr. Samuel Hayden/Seraphim in Doom and Doom Eternal, Reinhardt in Overwatch and Overwatch 2, Ardyn Izunia in Final Fantasy XV, Revenant in Apex Legends, Emperor Valkorion in Star Wars: The Old Republic, and J. Jonah Jameson in the Marvel’s Spider-Man series. Description above from the Wikipedia article Darin De Paul, licensed under CC-BY-SA, full list of contributors on Wikipedia.
