Gerry South

Gerry South؛ بازیگر مرتبط با ددپول ۲. مشاهده نقش‌ها و فیلموگرافی در مسیر مارول.

Gerry is a working Vancouver actor who has numerous credits in film, television and theatre. He attended the Ryerson Theatre School in Toronto and has trained in Los Angeles with some of the city's top casting directors. A student of acting , he constantly seeks to challenge himself to grow his skills. Gerry South is known for Deadpool 2 (2018), Beyond the Black Rainbow (2010) and Jeremiah (2002).
