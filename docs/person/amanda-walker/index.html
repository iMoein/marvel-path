Amanda Walker

Amanda Walker؛ بازیگر مرتبط با کاپیتان آمریکا: نخستین انتقام‌جو. مشاهده نقش‌ها و فیلموگرافی در مسیر مارول.

Amanda Walker (born 29 November 1935, Glasgow, Scotland) is a British actress, daughter of actress Madeleine Christie, who has appeared in over 100 productions since 1959. In film, she has appeared in Heat and Dust (1983), A Room with a View (1986), The English Patient (1996), Cloud Atlas (2012), and Triangle of Sadness (2022). On television she has had roles in Upstairs, Downstairs, Agatha Christie's Poirot, Sense8 (2017–2018), and Doctor Who (2024). She is married to actor Patrick Godfrey (m. 1960); they have two children.
