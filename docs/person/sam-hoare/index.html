Sam Hoare

Sam Hoare؛ بازیگر مرتبط با کاپیتان آمریکا: نخستین انتقام‌جو. مشاهده نقش‌ها و فیلموگرافی در مسیر مارول.

Sam Hoare (b. 27 June 1981) is a British actor and director best known for his role as Dickie Burnell, alongside Matt Smith in BBC1's Olympic drama Bert &amp; Dickie (2012) Hoare wrote and directed his début feature film, Having You, starring Anna Friel and Romola Garai, which was premièred in May 2013 on Sky Movies.
