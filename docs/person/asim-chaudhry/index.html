Asim Chaudhry؛ بازیگر مرتبط با نگهبانان کهکشان ۳. مشاهده نقش‌ها و فیلموگرافی در مسیر مارول.

Asim Chaudhry (Punjabi: عاصم چودھری) is an English comedian, writer, director and actor best known for playing Chabuddy G in the BBC mockumentary series People Just Do Nothing, which he co-created. For this role, he won a Royal Television Society Award and was nominated for two British Academy Television Awards.
