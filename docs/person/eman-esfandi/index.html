Eman Esfandi

Eman Esfandi؛ بازیگر مرتبط با مرد عنکبوتی: روزی نو. مشاهده نقش‌ها و فیلموگرافی در مسیر مارول.

Eman Esfandi (born September 27, 1994) is an American actor. He is best known for portraying the Jedi Ezra Bridger in the Disney+ series Ahsoka (2023-present). He also had small roles in King Richard (2021), The Inspection (2022) and Spider-Man: Brand New Day (2026). He is of Iranian and Ecuadorian descent.
