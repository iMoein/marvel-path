Dorothy Steel

Dorothy Steel؛ بازیگر مرتبط با پلنگ سیاه، پلنگ سیاه: واکاندا تا ابد. مشاهده نقش‌ها و فیلموگرافی در مسیر مارول.

Dorothy Steel (February 23, 1926 – October 15, 2021) was an American film and television actress who was known for starting her career in the industry at the age of 88, appearing in Hollywood films including Black Panther, Poms, Jumanji: The Next Level and her last film was Black Panther: Wakanda Forever.
