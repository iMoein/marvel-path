Clare Dunne

Clare Dunne؛ بازیگر مرتبط با مرد عنکبوتی: دور از خانه. مشاهده نقش‌ها و فیلموگرافی در مسیر مارول.

Clare Dunne is an Irish actress, born in Dublin. She has appeared in stage roles with the Abbey Theatre and the National Theatre. Dunne's film work includes the shorts The Cherishing (2016) and Nice Night for It (2017). Dunne portrayed Victoria in Spider-Man: Far From Home (2019). She then co-wrote and starred as Sandra in Herself (2020), which reunited her with director Phyllida Lloyd.
