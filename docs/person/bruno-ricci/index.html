Bruno Ricci

Bruno Ricci؛ بازیگر مرتبط با کاپیتان آمریکا: نخستین انتقام‌جو. مشاهده نقش‌ها و فیلموگرافی در مسیر مارول.

Bruno Ricci is an French actor and and production manager who portrayed Jacques Dernier in Captain America: The First Avenger. He is well-known for playing roles in movies such as 3 Days to Kill, The Apartment, and My Afternoons with Margueritte.
