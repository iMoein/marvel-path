Johnny Myers

Johnny Myers؛ بازیگر مرتبط با مرد عنکبوتی: روزی نو. مشاهده نقش‌ها و فیلموگرافی در مسیر مارول.

Johnny Myers is an American-born actor and singer based in the United Kingdom. Originally working as a jazz vocalist, he moved to London in 1970 and transitioned into acting through West End and National Theatre stage productions. Myers' screen credits include feature films such as Hackers (1995), Laws of Attraction (2004), The Marksman (2005), and Kon-Tiki (2012), along with television projects like Hiroshima (2005) and Sexy Beast (2024). He also portrayed a building manager in the superhero feature Spider-Man: Brand New Day (2026).
