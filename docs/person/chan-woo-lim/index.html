Chan Woo Lim؛ بازیگر مرتبط با انتقام‌جویان: عصر اولتران. مشاهده نقش‌ها و فیلموگرافی در مسیر مارول.

Chan Woo Lim is a British screen actor born in Seoul, South Korea. After immigrating to United Kingdom in 2002, he graduated with a National Diploma in Acting from Kingston College in 2011. He is also fluent in both English & Korean. He is known for Holby City (2021), Halo (2022), Killing Eve (2019) and Enterprice (2018).
