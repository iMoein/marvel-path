Angela Russo-Otstot

Angela Russo-Otstot؛ بازیگر مرتبط با کاپیتان آمریکا: سرباز زمستان. مشاهده نقش‌ها و فیلموگرافی در مسیر مارول.

Angela Russo-Otstot (born 1978) is an American screenwriter and film producer. She is the chief creative officer (CCO) at AGBO. She is also known for her collaboration with her brothers, directors Anthony and Joe Russo. She co-wrote the film Cherry (2021), produced the films Extraction 2 (2023) and The Electric State (2025), and executive produced the Prime Video television series Citadel and its spinoffs.
