Christiane Amanpour

Christiane Amanpour؛ بازیگر مرتبط با مرد آهنی ۲. مشاهده نقش‌ها و فیلموگرافی در مسیر مارول.

Christiane Maria Heideh Amanpour CBE is a British-Iranian journalist and television host. Amanpour is the Chief International Anchor for CNN and host of CNN International's nightly interview program Amanpour. She is also the host of Amanpour & Company on PBS.
