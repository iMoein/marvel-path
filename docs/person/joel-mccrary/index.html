Joel McCrary؛ بازیگر مرتبط با ثور. مشاهده نقش‌ها و فیلموگرافی در مسیر مارول.

Joel P. McCrary is an American actor and voice actor. His Disney roles include Bobby Wasabi in Kickin' It (which he was also the staff writer of the show), additional voices in the Cars video games Cars Race-O-Rama and Cars - The Video Game and the English version of Howl's Moving Castle, Prime Minister Motaz in The Princess Diaries (and its sequel), and Randy Szalinski in Honey, I Shrunk the Kids: The TV Show.
