Florence Kasumba؛ بازیگر مرتبط با کاپیتان آمریکا: جنگ داخلی، پلنگ سیاه، انتقام‌جویان: جنگ ابدیت، پلنگ سیاه: واکاندا تا ابد. مشاهده نقش‌ها و فیلموگرافی در مسیر مارول.

Florence Kasumba (born October 26, 1976) is a German actress of Ugandan origin. She is known for playing the role of Ayo in Captain America: Civil War, Black Panther (2018), Avengers: Infinity War (2018), The Falcon and the Winter Soldier (2021) and Black Panther: Wakanda Forever (2022). She also played Senator Acantha in Wonder Woman, and the Wicked Witch of the East in the NBC television series Emerald City.
