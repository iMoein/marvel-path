Sumela Kay

Sumela Kay؛ بازیگر مرتبط با مردان ایکس. مشاهده نقش‌ها و فیلموگرافی در مسیر مارول.

Sumela Kay was born on July 25, 1986 in Toronto, Ontario, Canada as Sumela-Rose Keramidopulos. She is an actress, known for A History of Violence (2005), X-Men(2000) and Simon Birch (1998).
