Logan Marshall-Green (born November 1, 1976, Charleston, South Carolina) is an American actor and filmmaker, the son of two drama teachers — his mother Lowry Marshall was the artistic director of Brown University's repertory theatre department. He was raised in Cranston, Rhode Island, has a twin brother named Taylor, and attended Barrington High School before earning a BA in Literature from the University of Tennessee, Knoxville, where he wrote for the school newspaper. He then attended the National Theater Institute in Waterford, Connecticut, and earned an MFA from New York University's Tisch School of the Arts. He was in a relationship with actress Marisa Tomei from 2008 to 2013, and was married to actress Diane Gaeta from 2012 to 2020; they have a son, Tennessee (born 2014). He made his directorial and writing debut with Adopt a Highway (2019) and won the Golden Joystick Award for Best Performer for his leading role in the 2019 video game Telling Lies. His notable film credits include The Great Raid (2005) (James Franco, Samuel L. Jackson), Across the Universe (2007) (Evan Rachel Wood, Joe Cohen), Brooklyn's Finest (2010) (Ethan Hawke, William Fichtner), Devil (2010) (Eiza González, Eric Bana), Prometheus (2012) (Noomi Rapace, Michael Fassbender), As I Lay Dying (2013) (Jessica Chastain, Garrett Hedlund), Madame Bovary (2014) (Isabelle Huppert, Ewan McGregor), The Invitation (2015) (Emily Blunt, Elizabeth Olsen), Snowden (2016) (Joseph Gordon-Levitt, Shailene Woodley), Sand Castle (2017) (Peyton List, David Oyelowo), Spider-Man: Homecoming (2017) (Tom Holland, Michael Keaton), Upgrade (2018) (Betty Gabriel, Melville J. Barlow), How It Ends (2021) (Theo James, Jurnee Smollett), Intrusion (2021) (Tessa Thompson, Luke Evans), Redeeming Love (2022) (Stana Katic, Jonathan Jackson), Lou (2022) (Jason Momoa, Tessa Thompson), Carry-On (2024) (Tessa Thompson, Anthony Mackie), Killing Castro (2026) (Al Pacino, Diego Boneta), and The Odyssey (2026) (Matt Damon, Tom Holland). On television he has appeared in The O.C. (2005), 24 (2005), Traveler (2007), Dark Blue (2009–2010), Quarry (2016), Damnation (2017–2018), When They See Us (2019), Big Sky (2021), and Marshals (2026).
