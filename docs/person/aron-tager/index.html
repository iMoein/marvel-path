Aron Tager

Aron Tager؛ بازیگر مرتبط با مردان ایکس. مشاهده نقش‌ها و فیلموگرافی در مسیر مارول.

Aron Tager was a versatile American-Canadian actor, voice artist, and sculptor. He is most fondly remembered by 90s kids for his recurring role as the eccentric "mad scientist" Dr. Vink in the Nickelodeon horror anthology series Are You Afraid of the Dark?
